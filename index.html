MechPro — The Complete Platform for Mobile Mechanics

MechPro is the all-in-one business management software built specifically for mobile mechanics and independent auto repair shops. From job dispatch and customer records to invoicing, payroll, and AI-powered diagnostics — everything you need to run a professional mobile repair operation is in one place.

Key Features

  • Job Management — Create, assign, and track repair orders from intake to completion. Log labor hours, parts used, and technician notes on every job.
  • GPS Tracking — See where your mobile mechanics are in real time. Dispatch the closest available technician and cut drive time between jobs.
  • Digital Invoicing — Generate professional invoices in seconds, collect digital signatures, and track payment status from your phone or desktop.
  • AI Diagnostics — Get instant repair cost estimates, AI-assisted diagnostics from vehicle symptoms, and smart scheduling suggestions.
  • Team Management — Manage employee profiles, certifications, schedules, and payroll with role-based access control.
  • Smart Scheduling — Book appointments with route optimization that minimizes travel time and avoids double-booking.
  • Parts Inventory — Keep a live parts inventory, log usage against repair orders, and get low-stock alerts before you run out of critical items.
